mass <- a * length ^ b

Run the function for the following:

  1. A Stegosauria that is 10 meters long.
  2. A Theropoda that is 8 meters long.
  3. A Sauropoda that is 12 meters long.
  4. An Ankylosauria that is 13 meters long.
